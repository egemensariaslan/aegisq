Style

Zero-noise extrapolation

⟨Z₀⟩ measured at each fold factor (U → U U† U), with three models extrapolated to λ = 0. Dashed line is the noiseless value at the same weights. Points and estimates are mean ± s.d. over independent random inputs — error bars, not one draw.

Gradient variance vs register width

Mean per-parameter gradient variance over random initialisations, log axis. Averaged across all circuit angles: rotations that commute through the measurement have a zero derivative regardless of width, and pinning one would distort the trend.

Signal retained under noise

Least-squares contraction factor f in Enoisy ≈ f · Eideal, at matched weights. 1.0 is unaffected, 0.0 is fully depolarised. Scale-free, so ansaetze with small expectation values are not flattered. Mean ± s.d. over independent random-input trials.

Symmetry verification

Particle-conserving circuit: gates preserve Hamming weight, so probability outside the sector is noise. Post-selection needs a full-distribution measurement rather than extra circuit runs; acceptance rate sets the shot overhead.

Training under a noise model

QuantumLayer in nn.Sequential, Adam, streamed per epoch. Epoch 0 is untrained.

Layer catalog and circuit cost

Per layer at the chosen width. Depth counts parallel two-qubit cycles; brickwork ansaetze stay at 2 per layer for any register size.

Known limits

  • LocalEntangler's default CZ entangler is diagonal, so a local_z read-out cannot fit globally correlated targets. On 4-qubit parity it reaches 0.569; entangler="cnot" or measurement="global_z" reaches 1.000 (run.py choose-ansatz).
  • PermutationEquivariant holds gradient variance at any width but has 3 parameters per layer; it scores 0.71–0.76 on two_moons against 0.94–0.97 for the baselines.
  • Extrapolation degrades above roughly p = 0.03 and can overshoot. Five closely spaced scale factors cost 129× the sampling variance of two.
  • Density-matrix simulation is 4n in memory: about 10–12 qubits here, fewer with measurement="probs".
